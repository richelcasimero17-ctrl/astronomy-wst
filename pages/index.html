ABOUT US

Welcome to Lakbay Kalawakan, a website created to help people explore and understand the wonders of the universe. Our goal is to make astronomy simple, interesting, and accessible to everyone. From planets and meteorites to the history of astronomy, our website provides basic information that allows visitors to discover how the universe works and how humans have studied it throughout history. Whether you are a student, an astronomy enthusiast, or simply curious about space, Lakbay Kalawakan is a place to learn, explore, and appreciate the vast universe around us.
